What the size brackets mean

Members disclose a range, never an amount. Every figure on this site keeps the range for that reason — a midpoint would invent precision the filing never had.

{% for letter, _floor, span in AMOUNT_BANDS %} {% endfor %}
{{ letter }} {{ span }}

The filing deadline is the same for every bracket: within 30 days of being notified of a trade, and no later than 45 days after it. Size changes what must be reported, not how fast — transactions under $1,000 need no report at all. Anything above shows a lag well past 45 days is a late filing, not a larger allowance.
