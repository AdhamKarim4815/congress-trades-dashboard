The score is shrunk toward zero in proportion to how little evidence backs it, so a member with four lucky trades does not outrank one with forty consistent ones. It is signed by intent: a sale that dodged a fall counts as much as a purchase that beat the index — which is exactly why buying and selling are ranked separately here. A member can rank near the top of the combined list without ever having bought anything. None of this is advice, and a rank measured on the past is not a promise about the next filing — what the measurement does not prove is written down.
